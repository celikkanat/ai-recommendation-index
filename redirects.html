When the AI suggests another way to buy

We ask ChatGPT and Gemini the same US buying questions and count the brands they recommend. Some answers do more than pick a brand: they suggest a used chair, single pans instead of a set, or a refurbished phone. This page counts how often that happened.

Four categories, measured October 28 to November 8, 2026. Updated 2026-11-09.
  • Office chairs. Gemini: 15 of 25 answers mentioned or suggested a used or refurbished chair. ChatGPT: 0 of 25.
  • Phones. 15 of 100 answers mentioned or suggested a refurbished or used phone (Gemini 9 of 50, ChatGPT 6 of 50).
  • Cookware sets. Gemini: 5 of 25 answers suggested single pieces instead of a set. ChatGPT: 0 of 25. Another 17 answers kept the set and suggested one extra pan; we do not count those.
  • Rowing machines. 1 of 50: one ChatGPT answer for a $500 budget said to look for a used Concept2.

These answers usually still name brands (a used Steelcase Leap is still a Steelcase), so a brand table alone does not show them. What changes is what the buyer is told to buy.
